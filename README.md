This repository presents a Power BI–driven analysis of Eco Drive Motors’ performance and sustainability between 2022–2025.
The project explores operations and logistics management through four balanced KPIs, combining financial performance with environmental responsibility.

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
Sales Mix by Vehicle Type

Balanced portfolio across Petrol, Diesel, and Electric (~£100M each)

Electric vehicles show rapid growth despite infrastructure barriers

Revenue Contribution by Region

Strong presence in Europe & coastal North America

Emerging markets show untapped potential

Customer Acquisition & Retention

Sales evenly distributed across reps (~20% each)

High-value customers drive premium model revenue

Carbon Footprint Reduction

Emissions split balanced across vehicle types

Model C most effective in emission avoidance

📈 Dashboard Features
Interactive Power BI dashboards with slicers for year, model, and region

Visualizations include maps, gauge charts, and longitudinal trend analysis

Real-time insights for strategic decision-making

🧭 Recommendations
Incentivize Electric & Hybrid vehicle adoption

Strengthen regional dealership networks in underperforming markets

Phase out low-efficiency Diesel models

Invest in EV charging partnerships with renewable energy providers

Enhance customer reward schemes and premium model marketing

🌍 Sustainability Alignment
Supports ESG and 2030 objectives

Tracks emissions reduction progress

Improves brand image through sustainability KPIs
